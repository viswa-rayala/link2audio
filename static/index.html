Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download an Instagram Reel or Post?

Copy any public Instagram Reel or Post URL (e.g. instagram.com/reel/... or instagram.com/p/...) from the app or web browser, paste it into the converter, and click Fetch. You can download it as an MP4 video or extract its audio as an MP3.

How do I convert a Spotify song?

Simply copy the Spotify track link (e.g. from the Spotify app or web player by clicking Share → Copy Song Link) and paste it into the converter. Link2Audio automatically resolves the song metadata and converts it into a high-quality MP3.

Can I download MP4 video as well as MP3 audio?

Yes! Switch between the "MP3 Audio" and "MP4 Video" toggle buttons on the preview card to download your media in your preferred format.

Do I need to install any extension or create an account?

No. Link2Audio is 100% web-based. There are no extensions to install, no accounts to create, and no cookies required.

What audio bitrates can I choose from?

You can choose between 320 kbps (Studio Quality), 256 kbps (High Fidelity), 192 kbps (Standard), or 128 kbps (Compact size).

Does this work on smartphones and tablets?

Yes. The site is fully responsive and optimized for iPhones, Android devices, iPads, tablets, and desktop browsers.
